v0.45.0-beta, the first beta. It works on Photoshop 2027 (28.0), which draws native buttons with its own grey face: every button in the panel is now drawn by the plugin and looks the same on Photoshop 2025 and 2027. Since v0.37.0 it also added Remove object (in Inpaint), Select by Text, Camera Angle (experimental) and a SeedVR2 restore option in Upscale. Upscaling a layer now opens the result at full size in its own document, results land at the right size in documents that are not 72 ppi, and Live Painting works on a starter install. Full notes: https://github.com/MehranMarxian/OpenLayer/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
